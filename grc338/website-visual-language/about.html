Welcome to our bagel shop, right here in the heart of Massachusetts, where the bagels are wicked fresh and the vibes are always good. We’re your classic neighborhood spot — the place you swing by in the morning half-awake, and leave with a hot coffee, a warm bagel, and a better attitude about life.

We started this place for one reason: Boston deserves real bagels. None of that store-bought, floppy nonsense. We’re talkin’ hand-rolled, kettle-boiled, baked-fresh-every-morning bagels that actually taste like something. If you grew up around here, you know—there’s nothing like a proper New England bagel to get your day goin’.

Around here, community matters. We know our regulars by name (and by order), we support local teams and local makers, and we always keep things friendly, fast, and wicked local. Whether you’re grabbing a breakfast sandwich before hopping on the Pike, stopping in after a Sox game, or fueling up before class, we’ve got you.

Thanks for supporting a small Massachusetts shop and keeping this place buzzing. Come by anytime—we’ll have the grill hot, the cream cheese whipped, and your go-to bagel waiting.
